# Third-Party Vendor Security Assessment Questionnaire

**Target Asset:** Cloud-based Payment Gateway Integration
**Framework Alignment:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 & India DPDP Act, 2023

## 1. Data Governance & Privacy (DPDP Act Alignment)
*   **Question 1.1:** Do you process or store Personal Identifiable Information (PII) of Indian citizens outside the geographical boundaries of India? If yes, specify the jurisdictions.
*   **Question 1.2:** What mechanisms do you have in place to permanently delete principal data once the specified business purpose is fulfilled? (DPDP Sec. 8).

## 2. Infrastructure & Access Control (ISO 27001 Alignment)
*   **Question 2.1:** Are your database clusters encrypted at rest using AES-256? Provide configuration or audit proof. (ISO Control A.8.24).
*   **Question 2.2:** Is Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) mandatory for all administrative access to your production environment? (ISO Control A.5.15).

## 3. Incident Response & Vulnerability Management
*   **Question 3.1:** What is your SLA for notifying our organization in the event of a data breach? (Maximum allowed: 72 hours).
*   **Question 3.2:** Provide your latest third-party Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) executive summary report.
